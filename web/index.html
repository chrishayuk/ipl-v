The IPL computer

IPL-V was the language of the first artificial intelligence programs, and its 1964 manual defines a machine: cells that each hold a word P Q SYMB LINK, a list of the cells not in use, and a cycle that interprets lists of instructions. This is that machine. Step a program and watch it take cells from available space, link them into lists and give them back.

The deck: one card to a word. ● stops there.
